On September 17, Etihad Airways (EY/ETD) brought back its last remaining grounded Airbus A380-800, which had been parked at Lourdes-Tarbes Airport since December 2020. The aircraft spent nearly six years at the French airport before flying to Abu Dhabi just a few days ago, where it is expected to resume commercial operations shortly.
